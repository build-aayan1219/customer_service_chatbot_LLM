Product Support Knowledge Base

Product: SmartHome Air Purifier X1

1. Product Overview
The SmartHome Air Purifier X1 is designed for home and office air purification. It includes three fan speeds, an automatic mode, a sleep mode, and a filter replacement indicator.

2. Filter Replacement
The HEPA filter should normally be replaced every 6 months. Heavy usage or very dusty environments may require earlier replacement. When the filter indicator turns red, inspect and replace the filter.

3. Warranty
The SmartHome Air Purifier X1 includes a 1-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects. Physical damage and damage caused by incorrect installation are not covered.

4. Customer Support
Customers can request product support through the customer service team. They should provide their order ID and a description of the issue.

5. Common Issue: Device Will Not Turn On
First, check that the power cable is firmly connected. Try a different wall outlet. If the device still does not turn on, contact customer support with the order ID.

6. Common Issue: Unusual Noise
Check whether the purifier is placed on a stable, level surface. Inspect the filter compartment for loose objects. If unusual noise continues, contact customer support.

7. Operating Modes
Automatic mode adjusts fan speed based on detected air quality. Sleep mode operates the purifier at a lower noise level for nighttime use.

8. Refund Requests
Refund requests should include the order ID and purchase details. The support team reviews refund eligibility according to the applicable purchase policy.
